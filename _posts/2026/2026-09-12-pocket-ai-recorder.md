Pocket is a small dedicated AI recorder that attaches magnetically to the back of a compatible phone or can be carried separately. A single button starts and stops recording, allowing you to capture conversations without opening an application or interrupting the discussion.

After recording, Pocket synchronizes the audio to its companion software and creates searchable transcripts, summaries, action items, and other AI-generated notes. Pocket currently supports iPhone, Android, Windows, macOS, and web access.

In this blog post, we will review the Pocket AI recorder, its companion applications, free and Pro features, and whether it is worth buying!

## Pocket Hardware

Pocket is designed to be small enough to keep attached to a phone or carry throughout the day.

Current hardware features include:

- 64GB of onboard storage
- Two microphones for in-person recording
- A contact microphone for phone calls
- MagSafe compatibility
- Approximately four days of typical battery life
- USB-C charging and data transfer
- Offline recording
- Recording range of up to approximately 15 meters under suitable conditions

Pocket records directly to its internal storage, so your phone does not need to remain connected while recording. The audio can be synchronized later when you reconnect to the Pocket application.

<br>

## Recording Meetings and Conversations

Using Pocket is intentionally simple.

Press the side button to begin recording and press it again when finished. A light and vibration provide confirmation that recording has started.

This is one of the biggest advantages of dedicated AI recording hardware.

Instead of:

- Unlocking your phone
- Finding a recording application
- Starting a voice memo
- Leaving the phone sitting on a table

you can simply press one button and continue the conversation.

Pocket is especially useful for:

- Meetings
- Interviews
- Client discussions
- Brainstorming
- Personal notes
- Lectures
- Phone calls
- Follow-up tasks

Always obtain appropriate consent before recording conversations and follow the recording laws and policies that apply to your location and organization.

<br>

## Phone Call Recording

Pocket includes a contact microphone designed to capture phone calls when the recorder is attached to the back of your phone.

This provides an advantage over many traditional voice recorders because the device can be used for both:

- In-person conversations
- Phone conversations

The same recording can then be processed into a transcript, summary, and action items.

Call-recording quality can vary depending on the phone, case, speaker volume, and how securely Pocket is positioned against the device.

<br>

## Pocket Companion Apps

Pocket is no longer limited to a mobile application.

The platform currently supports:

- iPhone
- Android
- macOS
- Windows
- Web access

The desktop application is particularly useful for people who spend most of the workday at a computer.

Pocket can also record online meetings such as:

- Zoom
- Microsoft Teams
- Google Meet

through its desktop software on Windows and macOS.

This makes Pocket more useful as an overall meeting companion rather than only a physical recorder.

<br>

## Synchronizing Recordings

Pocket provides several ways to transfer recordings.

You can synchronize using:

- Bluetooth
- Wi-Fi
- USB-C
- Pocket Web Sync
- Pocket Desktop

Bluetooth works well for normal short recordings.

For longer meetings, Wi-Fi synchronization can transfer recordings considerably faster. USB-C Web Sync is useful for large amounts of audio or when you want to transfer recordings directly to a computer.

<br>

## AI Notes and Summaries

After a recording is synchronized, Pocket can generate:

- Full transcription
- Meeting summaries
- Action items
- To-do items
- Mind maps
- Searchable notes
- Speaker information
- Follow-up information

Pocket supports more than 120 languages and can use multiple AI models for processing conversations.

One of the most useful features is being able to focus on the conversation instead of trying to write everything down.

You can review the transcript afterward and concentrate on the decisions, tasks, and details that actually mattered.

<br>

## Free Plan

Pocket's free tier is one of the biggest reasons the device stands out.

As of August 2026, Pocket advertises:

- Unlimited recording
- Unlimited transcription
- Unlimited summaries
- No transcription-minute cap
- No mandatory subscription

The current free plan retains conversation history for **14 days**, so important recordings should be exported or otherwise retained if you do not subscribe to Pro.

This is significantly different from AI recorders that provide only a few hundred free transcription minutes each month.

<br>

## Pocket Pro

Pocket Pro adds more advanced AI and long-term organization features.

Current Pro features include:

- Unlimited summaries saved permanently
- Higher-accuracy transcription
- Automatic speaker-name detection
- Unlimited Ask Pocket
- Access to more capable AI models
- Custom summary templates
- Summary regeneration
- Unlimited to-do items
- Advanced integrations
- File attachments
- Mind map export
- Additional export formats
- Raw audio downloads

At the time of this review, Pocket advertises Pro at **$199 per year**, equivalent to approximately $16.59 per month when billed annually. Pricing can change, so check the [Pocket website](https://heypocket.com/) before subscribing.

## Find Pocket

Because Pocket is small, losing it is a legitimate concern.

Pocket added a **Find Pocket** feature in 2026.

Open:

**Settings > Find Pocket**

The feature can provide:

- The last known location on a map
- Bluetooth proximity guidance when nearby
- Separation notifications when you leave Pocket behind

This is a welcome addition for a device that can easily disappear into a bag, vehicle, office, or couch.

<br>

## Privacy and Storage

Pocket stores recordings on its 64GB internal storage and can synchronize them with Pocket's cloud services.

During setup, Pocket also provides a local-only storage option for users who do not want cloud synchronization.

Because recordings may contain sensitive conversations:

- Enable strong account security.
- Keep the Pocket application updated.
- Review cloud-storage settings.
- Delete recordings that are no longer needed.
- Be cautious with confidential business conversations.
- Follow organizational recording policies.
- Obtain consent before recording other people.

For particularly sensitive environments, review Pocket's current privacy and data-processing policies before deploying the device.

## Advantages

Pocket's biggest advantages include:

- Unlimited transcription on the free tier
- No mandatory subscription
- One-button recording
- Phone-call and in-person recording
- 64GB internal storage
- Offline recording
- Multi-day battery life
- Mobile, desktop, and web applications
- Windows and macOS support
- Meeting summaries and action items
- Find Pocket functionality
- Multiple synchronization methods

The unlimited free transcription is especially attractive compared with competitors that impose relatively small monthly minute limits.

<br>

## Limitations

Pocket still has a few drawbacks:

- The free plan retains only 14 days of conversation history.
- Advanced AI features require Pro.
- Pro is relatively expensive at approximately $199 per year.
- Transcription accuracy depends on recording quality.
- Background noise and distance can affect results.
- Cloud AI processing may not be appropriate for every organization.
- The small device can be easy to misplace.
- Dedicated hardware is unnecessary for people who rarely record conversations.

The value depends heavily on how frequently you attend meetings or need to capture conversations.

<br>

## Is Pocket Worth It?

For someone who regularly participates in meetings, phone calls, interviews, or detailed conversations, **Pocket is one of the more interesting AI recorders available**.

The biggest advantage is its free core experience.

Many competing devices provide good hardware but make the recorder significantly less useful without a recurring subscription. Pocket's unlimited transcription allows you to continue using the core recording and note-taking functionality without paying every month.

I would start with the free plan.

Use Pocket for several weeks and determine whether you actually need:

- Permanent conversation history
- More powerful AI models
- Advanced exports
- Speaker identification
- Custom templates
- Deeper integrations

If those features become important, Pro is available. If not, the hardware remains useful without it.

<br>

## Overall Rating

**Rating: 9/10**

Pocket combines simple hardware with a surprisingly capable AI companion platform.

The ability to press one button, record an entire conversation, and later receive a transcript, summary, action items, and searchable notes makes it useful for anyone who spends a significant amount of time in meetings or on calls.

The strongest selling point is the unlimited transcription included with the free plan. While Pocket Pro adds valuable advanced features, the device does not become useless if you decide not to maintain another subscription.

For that reason, Pocket is particularly compelling for users who want an AI recorder but are tired of paying recurring fees simply to transcribe their own recordings.

<br>

## Conclusion

Pocket is more than a traditional digital voice recorder. Its hardware captures conversations while its companion software turns those recordings into organized and searchable information.

With support for in-person meetings, phone calls, desktop meetings, mobile devices, Windows, macOS, and web access, Pocket can fit into a wide range of workflows.

The optional Pro subscription provides more advanced AI capabilities, but the unlimited free transcription makes the core device useful on its own. For frequent meetings and phone calls, Pocket is an excellent AI note-taking companion.
