Walmart's **onn 4K Pro Streaming Device** provides many of the features found in substantially more expensive streaming boxes while remaining relatively affordable.

The 2026 model runs Google TV with Gemini and includes 32GB of storage, 3GB of RAM, Wi-Fi 6, Ethernet, Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, and a backlit voice remote with a remote-finder feature.

At approximately $60, it sits at the top of Walmart's onn streaming lineup but remains considerably less expensive than many premium streaming devices.

In this blog post, we will review the onn 4K Pro Streaming Device and discuss why finding one in stock can sometimes be the hardest part.

## Key Features

The 2026 onn 4K Pro includes:

- 4K Ultra HD output
- Google TV with Gemini
- Dolby Vision
- Dolby Atmos
- 32GB of internal storage
- 3GB of RAM
- Wi-Fi 6
- Ethernet connectivity
- Google Cast
- Backlit voice remote
- Find My Remote functionality
- Hands-free voice control
- Google Play Store access

The additional storage is particularly useful compared with inexpensive streaming sticks that provide only 8GB or 16GB.

More storage means more room for streaming applications, utilities, games, IPTV applications, and other Google TV software.

<br>

## Google TV Interface

One of the strongest reasons to choose an onn device is Google TV.

Google TV provides access to applications such as:

- Netflix
- YouTube
- Disney+
- Hulu
- Prime Video
- Max
- Plex
- Kodi
- TiviMate
- VLC

Applications can be installed directly from the Google Play Store.

Google TV also combines content recommendations from supported streaming services onto the home screen.

Users who prefer a simpler interface can customize the home screen, rearrange applications, reduce recommendations, and configure an apps-focused layout.

<br>

## Performance

The 3GB of RAM provides enough memory for smooth everyday navigation and app switching.

For normal streaming use, the device handles:

- 4K streaming
- IPTV applications
- Plex
- YouTube
- Live television
- Local media playback
- Multiple installed applications

The 32GB of storage is one of the Pro model's biggest advantages.

It provides considerably more breathing room for applications than many budget streaming devices and reduces the constant storage-management problems common with 8GB devices.

<br>

## Remote Control

The included remote is another highlight.

Features include:

- Voice control
- Backlit buttons
- Dedicated streaming-service buttons
- Programmable controls
- Find My Remote

The remote finder is particularly useful. Instead of searching couches and furniture for a missing remote, the device can trigger an audible alert from the remote.

This sounds like a minor feature until you actually need it.

<br>

## Ethernet and Wi-Fi 6

The Pro model includes both Wi-Fi 6 and an Ethernet port.

Wi-Fi is sufficient for most installations, but Ethernet is valuable when:

- Streaming high-bitrate 4K content
- Using IPTV
- Playing media from a local Plex server
- Wi-Fi coverage is inconsistent
- The streaming device is installed near a network switch

Having Ethernet built into the device eliminates the need for a separate USB Ethernet adapter.

<br>

## onn 4K Pro vs. 4K Plus

Walmart also sells the onn 4K Plus.

The 4K Plus provides:

- 16GB of storage
- 2GB of RAM
- Dolby Vision
- Dolby Atmos
- Wi-Fi 6
- Google TV

The Pro increases this to:

- 32GB of storage
- 3GB of RAM
- Ethernet
- Backlit remote
- Remote finder
- Hands-free voice features

If the price difference is reasonable, I prefer the **4K Pro**.

The additional storage, memory, Ethernet port, and better remote make it a more complete long-term streaming device.

<br>

## The Biggest Problem: Finding One

As of **August 2026**, one of the biggest disadvantages of the onn 4K Pro is simply finding one.

Walmart's onn streaming devices have had unusually inconsistent availability.

You may see:

- Out of stock online
- Shipping unavailable
- Pickup unavailable
- One Walmart showing stock while another does not
- Devices appearing in stores before the website reflects the inventory
- Small quantities disappearing quickly

Walmart also does not always launch onn streaming hardware through a traditional nationwide release. New units have historically appeared gradually as distribution centers send inventory to individual stores.

Because of this, checking only Walmart.com may not tell the entire story.

<br>

## Check Multiple Walmart Stores

If Walmart's website says the onn 4K Pro is unavailable, it may be worth checking nearby stores manually.

Try:

- Changing the selected Walmart store in the app.
- Searching stores within a larger radius.
- Checking same-day pickup availability.
- Calling the electronics department.
- Visiting nearby Walmart locations.
- Checking the streaming-device shelves in person.

In some cases, inventory may be available at a physical store even when finding it online is difficult.

If you are already near several Walmart stores, browsing the electronics section can sometimes be more successful than repeatedly refreshing the website.

Availability changes frequently, so this is particularly important when the Pro model has just been restocked.

The onn 4K Pro is a strong option for anyone who wants:

- Google TV without spending $100 or more.
- Plenty of application storage.

## Overall Rating

**Rating: 9/10**

The onn 4K Pro is one of the better-value Google TV streaming devices available.

The combination of 32GB of storage, 3GB of RAM, Ethernet, Wi-Fi 6, Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, Google TV, and an excellent remote makes it difficult to beat at approximately $60.

The primary frustration is availability. As of August 2026, finding one can require checking Walmart regularly, changing your selected store, expanding your search radius, or physically visiting nearby Walmart locations.

If you can find one at the normal retail price, the onn 4K Pro is an easy recommendation.

<br>

## Conclusion

The 2026 onn 4K Pro proves that a capable Google TV streaming device does not need to cost $100 or $200.

It provides the features most users actually need, including fast 4K streaming, ample storage, Ethernet, Wi-Fi 6, Google Play applications, Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, and a very good remote.

Finding one may require more effort than purchasing a Roku or Fire TV device, but the combination of price and functionality makes the search worthwhile.
